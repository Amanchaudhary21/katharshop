      About this demo

      ShopNest is a practice marketplace that shows how an online store works, from search to checkout.

      What you can try

      • Search, filter and sort products
      • Save items to your wish list
      • Fill a cart and use a coupon
      • Place a demo order and see it in Orders

      Store rules

      • Coupons: WELCOME10, FREESHIP
      • Your cart and orders stay in this browser only
